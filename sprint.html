FIT IT TO THE FIELD: TEAM HANGMAN

Every word in this sprint comes from Unit 6. Your site plays as one team against the other site. You race to solve terms like domain shift, shortcut, and the Fit Check, and you win bonus points by remembering what happened in the Unit 6 cases. Then each of you takes two of the words and fits them to your own field.

Open the sprint

Your instructor runs the game on one shared screen. If you want to follow along on your own laptop, copy this address into your web browser:

https://teamgalacticdisc.github.io/unit6-hangman/

The game runs in your browser. No installation and no account needed.

Before you start

  • Each site is one team: Yokota and Iwakuni.
  • Each team picks one caller. Talk it over as a team, then the caller says your letter or your answer out loud.
  • Turn on your sound. The game reads each clue, answer, and bonus question out loud.
  • Keep a piece of paper or a blank document open. You will write down the words as they are solved.

How the game works

MoveWhat happens
Guess a letterA right letter is worth 10 points each time it shows up, and your team keeps the turn. A wrong letter adds a piece to the AI robot and the turn passes to the other site.
SolveOn your turn, try the whole word. Right is worth 50. Wrong costs 20 and the turn passes.
StealOnce per game, solve the word on the other site's turn. Right is worth 50. Wrong costs 20.
LifelineOnce per game, reveal one letter for free on your turn.
Bonus questionThe team that wins the word gets one Unit 6 question worth 25.
Robot finishedEight wrong letters builds the whole robot. Nobody wins that word.

What your team does

StepWhat you do

5. Exit ticketOn your own, pick two words from tonight's game. For each word, write three things:
a. What it means, in your own words.
b. Which Fit Check question it belongs to: data, users, workflow, stakes, or rules.
c. One place it could show up in your own field or your capstone organization.
Sample exit ticket line. This sample is made up to show the format.
Alert fatigue. It is when people start ignoring alerts because most of them turn out to be wrong. Fit Check question: users. In my maintenance shop, if a corrosion tool flagged every panel, inspectors would stop looking at the flags, and the one real flag would get missed.

FOR THE INSTRUCTOR

WHAT TO TEACH BEFORE YOU PLAY

The game only rewards what the class already knows, so the 10 minute lesson matters. Every word and bonus question comes from the Unit 6 Guide. Teach these five topics in this order, and make sure the numbers in the right column come up, because the bonus questions ask about them.

Topic and where it isWords in the gameMake sure the class hears
1. Lab test, pilot, deployment
Guide Part 1, section 1		LAB TEST, PILOT, DEPLOYMENTWhat each step proves. A pilot shows the tool works in one place with attention on it. GAO's first fix was to put one office in charge.
2. The five cases
Guide Part 1, sections 2 to 6		EXTERNAL VALIDATION, ALERT FATIGUE, PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE, SUMMER MELT, POUNCE, WORKFLOWEye clinic: more than a fifth of photos rejected. Sepsis: Michigan studied 38,455 hospitalizations, the model missed 67% of cases. GAO made 16 recommendations, all about organizing, planning, and measuring. Pounce cost $7 to $15 per student and did nothing for students who had not committed. Zillow wrote down about $304 million.
3. Domain shift and shortcuts
Guide Part 2, section 1, and the Project Reference		DOMAIN SHIFT, SHORTCUTThe four ways a field can differ: equipment and conditions, population, definitions, time. The Mount Sinai X-ray model dropped from 0.802 to 0.717 at NIH. On this scale 0.50 is a coin flip.
4. Domain knowledge and the Fit Check
Guide Part 2, sections 2 to 4		DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE, FIT CHECK, STAKESThe five questions: data, users, workflow, stakes, rules. Answer stakes early because it sets the bar for the other four. RAND's most common root cause is a misunderstood problem. BMW needed about 100 images per feature.
5. Adapting a tool
Guide Part 2, sections 5 and 6		THRESHOLD, SENSITIVITY, SPECIFICITY, ANOMALY DETECTION, FEEDBACK LOOPThe five moves, and that moving the cutoff is free in the Transfer Test. Mayo's ECG model scored 0.93 in the lab and its trial raised new diagnoses 32%. A clinic asks if patients did better. A prediction model forecasts a number or event. Zillow skipped phase it in.
